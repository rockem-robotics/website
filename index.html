What we are

A booster club, run by parents, for the robotics students

Rock'Em Robotics Booster Club, also called the Lake Robotics Booster Club, supports the robotics students of Lake Local Schools — Lake High School, home of the Blue Streaks.

The club is parent- and community-funded. That is the whole model: families join, neighbors chip in, and the money goes to the students.

It also keeps the fundraising and the paperwork off the coaches' desks. Every hour a coach is not chasing money or filling out forms is an hour spent with the students, which is the job we want them doing.

The work on our side is unglamorous. The students do the part worth watching.
