Before you join

What you are signing up for

Three plain facts, and nothing we cannot back up.

It funds the booster club

Your $10 goes to the Rock'Em Robotics Booster Club, and the booster club exists to support the robotics students of Lake Local Schools. We are parent- and community-funded, so memberships are the money we have to work with.

It also keeps the fundraising off the coaches, so their time goes to the students instead.

It puts you on the list

When there is an event to staff, a fundraiser to run, or a job nobody has picked up yet, members are who we ask. Say yes when you can and no when you cannot. Nobody is keeping score, and a season where you only make two meetings still helps.

It covers your whole household

One membership covers everyone in your family for the 2026–27 season, so anyone at your address can come to a meeting, vote on what we do, and pitch in.

We would rather be straight with you: $10 is the smallest thing we are going to ask for. This club runs on parents who show up, and the drive is how we find out who is with us this season. If that sounds like more than you can take on right now, join anyway — the money still helps, and you can decide about the rest later.

Or give a one-time gift

If you would rather give once, in any amount, that link is separate from membership and it helps too. Membership is still the thing we are asking for first.
